# Lemon Blueberry Cake

## meta

- tags:
  - dessert
  - cake
  - baking
- servings: 10

## instructions

### init

- Preheat `# 350°F oven`
- Prepare a `# 26cm round cake pan`

### wet mixture

- In a bowl, beat `@ 2 eggs` with `@ 240g/1.25cups sugar` until light and fluffy
- Mix in `@ 0.5cup/120g milk`, `@ 0.5cup/120g greek joghurt`,
  `@ 3tbsp lemon juice`, `@ 1tbsp lemon zest`, `@ 1tbsp vanilla extract`

### cake

- In another bowl, beat `@ 0.75cup/170g softened butter` until creamy
- Sift in `@ 2cups/240g flour`, `@baking powder`, `@salt`
- Pour in the `wet mixture` and mix until just combined
- Coat `@ 200g blueberries` with `@ 1tbsp flour` and fold into the batter
- Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for `~45min`
- Let the cake cool in the pan for `~15min` before transferring to a wire rack
- Let it cool completely
