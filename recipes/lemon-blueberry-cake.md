# Lemon Blueberry Cake

## meta

- tags:
  - dessert
  - cake
  - baking
- servings: 10

## instructions

### init

- Preheat `# 350°F oven`
- Prepare a `# 26cm round cake pan`

### wet mixture

- In a bowl, beat `2 eggs` with `240g/1.25cups sugar` until light and fluffy
- Mix in `0.5cup/120g milk`, `0.5cup/120g greek joghurt`, lemon juice and zest
  of `2 lemons`, `vanilla extract`

### cake

- In another bowl, beat `0.75cup/170g softened butter` until creamy
- Sift in `2cups/240g flour`, `baking powder`, `salt`
- Pour in the `wet mixture` and mix until just combined
- Coat `200g blueberries` with `1tbsp flour` and fold into the batter
- Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for `~45min`
- Let the cake cool in the pan for `~15min` before transferring to a wire rack
- Let it cool completely

### blueberry reduction

- In a saucepan, combine `200g blueberries`, `2tbsp sugar`, lemon juice and zest
  of `1 lemon`
- Cook over medium-low heat until the blueberries burst and the mixture is
  reduced by half
- Cool down completely

### cream

- In a bowl, whip `200g heavy cream` with `150g mascarpone` and `1tbsp sugar`
  until stiff peaks form
- Fold in the `blueberry reduction`
- Spread the cream over the cake and garnish with fresh blueberries
